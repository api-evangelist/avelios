## About this Website
topics:
  - Avelios is a modular, data-driven Hospital Information System (HIS 2.0).
  - Digitises the full patient journey: outpatient, inpatient, ER, OR, nursing, medication, labs, communication, administration, research.
  - Provides modules for patient portal, TI integration, device/app connectivity, and digital consults.
  - Includes administration tools: scheduling, occupancy, queue management, automated billing.
  - Offers research, BI dashboards, workflow automation, and diagnostic decision support.
  - Built on microservices (Spring Boot, Rust, PostgreSQL, React) with HL7, FHIR, DICOM, TI interoperability.
  - Available on-premise or cloud; DSGVO-compliant; secure with TLS 1.2/1.3, JWT, Argon2.
  - Enables hospitals to improve efficiency, data quality, patient care, and research capabilities.
